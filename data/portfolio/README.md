# Synthetic Wholesale Bank Portfolio (SYNTHETIC - NOT REAL CLIENT DATA)

`portfolio.csv` is generated by `python -m src.portfolio.build` with a fixed seed (`20260101`), so it is fully
reproducible. Every row is fictional: counterparty names come from the public risk universe
(`data/universe.csv`) purely to make exposures recognisable; no actual loan, bond, derivative or equity
holding of any institution is represented.

## Assumptions
- 50 positions, ~USD 1.3B market value: 18 loans, 12 bonds, 10 derivatives, 10 strategic equities.
- Sizes are uniform random draws within hand-set ranges per position (see `SPEC` in `src/portfolio/build.py`).
- Derivatives: `market_value` = 2% of notional (MTM exposure proxy). `delta` is the bank's sensitivity to the
  underlying: commodity swaps are short oil vs airlines (`-0.6`) and long vs producers (`+0.4..0.5`).
- Bond `duration` ~ 0.85 x maturity (capped at 9). Loans carry `collateral_pct` of 0-40% (trade finance 10-25%).
- `tags` (pipe-separated) are the exposure tags the stress engine matches against `shock_table.csv`.
- Concentrations are designed for the demo: shipping/trade finance, energy producers vs airlines, US/EU banks,
  semiconductors.

## shock_table.csv
Theme x tag shocks at reference severity (impact 8), scaled per signal by its calibrated move. Rows flagged
`source=measured` take equity moves from the event-study seed (`data/calibration/shock_seed.csv`, n >= 2);
all other values are `assumed` and documented in the `rationale` column.
